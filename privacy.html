Privacy Policy

SpellBee  ·  Last updated: September 2026

SpellBee is a spelling-practice app designed for children preparing for school spelling bees. Your privacy — and your child's privacy — is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

Nothing that leaves the device. SpellBee stores all practice data (words practiced, scores, streaks, mastered words) locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. No data is sent to our servers or any third party.

Subscription and payments

SpellBee offers an optional subscription (SpellBee Plus) through Apple's App Store. All billing is handled entirely by Apple. We never see, store, or process your payment information. For details on how Apple handles your data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

SpellBee contains no advertising of any kind.

Analytics and tracking

SpellBee does not use any analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services that send data off-device, or any form of tracking. We do not use Apple's App Analytics, Firebase, or any similar service.

Children's privacy

SpellBee is designed for use by children under parental supervision. Because we collect no personal information whatsoever, SpellBee complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international regulations.

Third-party services

SpellBee does not integrate any third-party SDKs beyond Apple's standard frameworks (StoreKit, AVFoundation). No data is shared with any third party.

Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app from your iPhone or iPad. We have no copy of it.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through an app update.
