Subscription and billing

SpellBee Plus subscriptions are billed through Apple. To manage, pause, or cancel your subscription:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions.
  • Find SpellBee and tap it to make changes.

You can also tap Manage subscription inside the SpellBee app under Settings.

Free trial

SpellBee Plus includes a 7-day free trial. You will not be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. A reminder notification is sent on day 5 to help you decide.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen. Your subscription will be restored as long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID that was used to subscribe.

Progress and data

All practice data — words mastered, streaks, session history — is stored locally on your device. It is not backed up to the cloud. Deleting the app will erase it permanently. We recommend keeping the app installed even if you pause your subscription, so your child's progress is preserved.

Word audio

SpellBee plays a pre-recorded pronunciation for each word. If a word's audio is missing for any reason, the app automatically falls back to your device's built-in speech — it will never be silent or crash.

Parental gate

SpellBee asks a mental-arithmetic question before any purchase, external link, or settings action. This ensures children cannot make accidental purchases or access external content without a parent present.

Privacy

SpellBee collects no personal data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
